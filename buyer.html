Frequently Asked Questions

If I buy 5 tickets, how many e‑tickets do I receive?
Exactly 5. Each one is a separate raffle entry with its own Ticket No. and Verification Code.
Can I buy several tickets?
Yes. The quantity you pay for is the quantity of official e‑tickets you receive.
Can I choose my raffle number?
Yes. Preferred numbers are optional and subject to seller confirmation.
What is the Control No.?
It identifies the whole purchase transaction. It does not replace your individual e‑tickets.
When do I receive my E‑Tickets?
After payment verification and seller ticket release.
